
## Rules

- Promo CTA is ALWAYS the same two sentences — consistency builds the habit
- Never say "I make money from this" — let the value speak
- Reply to every comment: "want the full pack?" → link
- 2–3 promos per week max; the rest organic (your blueprint scripts)
