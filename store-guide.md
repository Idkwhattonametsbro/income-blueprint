# OnePageStudio — Etsy Setup Guide

Complete step-by-step to open your shop and list the Calm Week System.
Total: ~30 minutes. Keep this tab open while you do it.

## Part 1 · Create the shop (10 min)

1. Go to **etsy.com** → tap **Sell on Etsy** (bottom) or **https://www.etsy.com/sell**
2. **Shop preferences:**
   - Shop language: **English**
   - Shop country: **Morocco**
   - Shop currency: **USD** (digital buyers worldwide pay in USD)
3. **Shop name:** `OnePageStudio` (must be unique — if taken, try `OnePageStudioCo` or `OnePageStudioShop`)
4. **About / tagline:** "Minimal printables for calmer weeks"
5. Finish the shop setup wizard (profile, photo = cover-product.png)

> ⚠️ **Watch for the Etsy Payments step.** If it says payments are available in
> your region → great, continue. If it says unavailable → stop and tell me,
> we'll use a different storefront that pays out via Payoneer.

> Do NOT include 00-cover in the ZIP — that's the listing cover.

## Part 3 · Create the listing (10 min)

Tap **Sell on Etsy** → **Listings** → **Add a listing**:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Title** (140 chars) | `Printable Weekly Planner A4 | Calm Week System | One Page Weekly Reset | Habit Tracker | Budget Tracker | Digital Download PDF` |
| **Category** | Paper & Party Supplies → Paper → Planners |
| **Type** | Digital |
| **Price** | 7.00 USD |
| **Quantity** | 999 (digital, unlimited) |
| **Description** | paste from `listings/etsy-listing.md` |
| **Tags** (13) | `weekly planner, printable planner, weekly reset, habit tracker printable, budget tracker, digital planner, calm productivity, adhd planner, student planner, pdf planner, minimal planner, sunday reset, planner bundle` |
| **Photos** | 1. `product/cover-product.png` (cover) 2. screenshot of 01 3. screenshot of 04 4. screenshot of 05 |
| **File** | upload `calm-week-system.zip` |

Then **Publish**.

## Part 4 · After it's live (5 min)

- Copy the listing URL → that's your link for your YouTube bio and video comments
- First 24h: check if Etsy shows the listing in search for "printable planner" / "weekly planner"
- One listing is the seed. The daily Etsy engine (income-blueprint, every morning)
  gives you a NEW product idea + listing text + cover — add one listing per day
  until you have 10. That's the compounding machine.

## Why this works

- Digital product = 100% margin, no shipping, sells while you sleep
- Calm Week System is in a proven category (planners/printables on Etsy)
- The engine feeds you a new proven product idea + ready listing every morning
