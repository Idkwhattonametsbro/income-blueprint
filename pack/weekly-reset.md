# The One-Page Weekly Reset (free lead magnet)

Make this in Google Docs (free), export to PDF, share via a Google Drive link.
One page. That's the whole product for now — it collects nothing, sells nothing,
and trains you to finish things.

---

## Page layout (one A4 / Letter page)

**Header:**
THE ONE-PAGE WEEK — 15 minutes every Sunday night

**Section 1 — BRAIN DUMP (2 minutes)**
"Write everything floating in your head. All of it. This page can hold it."

**Section 2 — THE 3 (2 minutes)**
"Circle the three things that actually matter this week."
[ ] 1. ____________
[ ] 2. ____________
[ ] 3. ____________

**Section 3 — THE ANCHOR (2 minutes)**
"One meal, one walk, one early night. One thing that keeps the week human."
[ ] Anchor: ____________

**Section 4 — MONEY + MEALS, ONE LINE EACH (3 minutes)**
"Predictable beats perfect. Write the plan, don't optimize it."
- Money this week: ____________
- Meals this week: ____________

**Section 5 — THE FUN THING (1 minute)**
"Put one fun thing in the calendar first. Not last. First."
- Fun: ____________

**Footer:**
"Do this Sunday night. Monday becomes a start, not a surprise."
