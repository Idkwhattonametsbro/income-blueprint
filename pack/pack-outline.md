# Pack Outline — the $7–9 digital pack (8–12 items, $0 tools)

Niche: calm weekly systems for broke beginners.
Rule: everything below is makeable with $0 tools (Google Docs/Sheets/Drive, Canva free, phone).
Selling starts only after people ask for the freebie. Until then: free download trains you to finish things.

## Pack name ideas

- "The One-Page Week"
- "Calm Week System"
- "The Broke-Student Reset Kit"

## The 8–12 items

1. **One-page weekly reset (printable PDF)** — the lead magnet, upgraded with a clean design
2. **The 1-3-5 daily sheet** — 1 big / 3 medium / 5 small, one page
3. **Sunday-night ritual checklist** — the 3 moves, tickbox version
4. **The shutdown ritual card** — print it, stick it by your bed
5. **Meal + money one-liner sheet** — weekly budget-without-interest tracker (no riba products)
6. **Exam / deadline countdown page** — the "deadline that scares you" tracker
7. **3-tab phone setup guide** — 2-page PDF, screenshots welcome
8. **20 hooks swipe file** — for people who make content (or keep as bonus)
9. **30-day calm streak chart** — one square a day, no app needed
10. **Fill-in-the-blank weekly plan** — for people who hate starting from zero
11. **The "Later" folder system** — one page explaining the 3-tab method
12. **Bonus: voice-script templates** — 3 scripts they can record themselves

## Making it (batch, ~1 weekend)

- Google Docs for text pages → export PDF (free)
- Google Sheets for trackers → export or screenshot (free)
- Canva free for the cover + any visual pages
- Consistent style: 2 colors max, one font family, lots of white space
- Every page: same header, same footer ("part of the Calm Week System")

## Honest pricing

- Free: the one-page weekly reset (lead magnet, pinned comment)
- $7–9: the full pack when people ask twice
- "Pay what you want" (min $0) on Payhip/Gumroad once you have an email list
- Bundle later: pack + 30-day challenge = $12–15

## Page list for the sales page (when it exists)

- Hook: "Your week is a mess because you have no system."
- What's inside: the 12 items, one line each
- Proof: 1–2 screenshots of the pages
- Price + guarantee: "if the first page doesn't help, it's free"
- CTA: "Get the pack — $7"

## Never include

- Fake income claims or testimonials
- Anything requiring interest, betting, adult, or scam-adjacent mechanisms
- Your real name if you prefer anonymity (brand name only)
