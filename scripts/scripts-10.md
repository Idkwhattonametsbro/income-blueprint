## Script 1 — The weekly reset (lead magnet)

[HOOK] "Your week is a mess because you have no system — here's the 5-minute fix."
15 minutes on Sunday night. That's the whole investment.
**Step 1: brain dump.** Write everything floating in your head on one page. The mess lives in your head, not your calendar.
**Step 2: pick 3.** Circle the three things that actually matter this week. Everything else is a bonus.
**Step 3: one anchor.** One meal, one walk, one early night. One thing that makes the week feel human.
That's the reset. One page. Free template pinned in the comments.
**Save this for Sunday.**

## Script 2 — The 1-3-5 rule

[HOOK] "Your to-do list is too long. Cut it to 3 things and watch what happens."
The 1-3-5 rule: **1 big thing, 3 medium things, 5 small things.** That's a realistic day.
Big = takes real focus, moves your life. Medium = 30 minutes. Small = quick wins.
If you finish the 1 and the 3, the day was a win. The 5 are just gravy.
**Your list isn't a promise — it's a menu.** Pick what fits the day.
**Follow for one calm week.**

## Script 3 — The 2-2-2 rule

[HOOK] "The 2-2-2 rule: the cheapest way to get a calmer week."
**2 hours a week for yourself.** No work, no phone. Boring is fine.
**2 hours for one person** who matters to you. Coffee, walk, call.
**2 hours for future you** — one small thing that makes next week easier.
That's 6 hours out of 168. You can afford it, even broke.
**Save this so you remember.**

## Script 4 — The shutdown ritual

[HOOK] "You don't need a new app. You need a shutdown ritual."
When does your day actually end? If the answer is "never", fix it tonight.
**Pick a time. At that time, 3 things:**
**1. Write tomorrow's 3 tasks.** Your brain can stop holding them.
**2. Close every tab — phone and brain.**
**3. One sentence: "Done for today."** Say it out loud.
Ten minutes. The end of the day becomes a door you close instead of a wall you fall through.
**Follow for calmer weeks.**

## Script 5 — The Sunday 15

[HOOK] "This Sunday ritual takes 15 minutes and saves you 5 hours."
Every Sunday night, same three moves:
**1. Clean slate** — wipe last week's list. Guilt doesn't live in this system.
**2. Plan meals + money** — one line each. Broke-friendly means predictable.
**3. Put one fun thing in the calendar first.** Not last. First.
Monday then feels like a start, not a surprise.
**The template is in my bio. Free.**

## Script 6 — The 3-tab phone setup

[HOOK] "Your phone is the problem. Here's the 3-tab setup that fixes it."
Most phones are a casino with a calendar. Here's the $0 fix:
**Tab 1: the only home screen.** Five apps max. Everything else lives in the app drawer.
**Tab 2: grayscale at night.** Settings → accessibility → display. It kills the dopamine loop.
**Tab 3: one folder called "Later".** Anything you can't do now goes there, not in your head.
One afternoon to set up. Every day after is calmer.
**Save this and set it up tonight.**

## Script 7 — The broke student's system

[HOOK] "How broke students actually get organized (no course, no app)."
You don't need Notion. You don't need a 47-step productivity guru system.
**One notebook, one pen, one page a day:**
**Top: today's 3.** **Middle: the deadline that scares you.** **Bottom: one line you're grateful for.**
Cost: about 20 cents a week.
The students who pass aren't more disciplined — they have a system small enough to keep.
**Follow for the next 15 days of this.**

## Script 8 — The 10-minute evening

[HOOK] "The 10-minute evening that makes tomorrow effortless."
Ten minutes before bed, four moves:
**1. Phone across the room.** You'll thank me at 2AM.
**2. Tomorrow's clothes out** (or bag packed if you study/work out).
**3. Tomorrow's 3 tasks written down.**
**4. Water on the nightstand.**
That's it. Future-you starts the day already winning.
**Save this. Do it tonight.**

## Script 10 — You're not the problem

[HOOK] "If you can't keep a habit for a week, you're not the problem. The system is."
Every habit system assumes you have time, energy, and a clean desk.
Broke, tired, and overwhelmed is a different game.
**Smaller than you think:** 3 tasks, 10-minute evening, one page.
**Slower than you want:** one habit at a time, two weeks minimum.
**Boring is the point.** Boring systems survive. Exciting ones die by Thursday.
**Follow for a calmer week — starting now.**
