# Batch Generator — 10 scripts in 60–90 minutes

The 90%: thinking and drafting are systematized. Your batch day is pick → record → export.

## The batch template (fill once, reuse forever)

For each of the 10 scripts, this skeleton produces a post in ~5 minutes:

```
[HOOK] — one sentence, first second, promise or tension
BODY — 3 steps or 3 ideas, one per breath (~8 seconds each)
[PROOF/FREEBIE] — "template in bio / link in comments"
CTA — "save this" or "follow for calm weeks"
```

## Batch day flow

1. **Pick 5 scripts** from `scripts-10.md` (or generate 10 fresh with your AI agent: "Batch 10 Shorts scripts, faceless, niche: [north star line]").
2. **Record voice** for each (one take, calm voice, ~50s).
3. **Add on-screen text** (bold lines only) in your free editor (CapCut).
4. **Drop in b-roll** from your phone (desk, walk, notebook, screen recording).
5. **Export 5 files**, 9:16, under 60s.
6. File them in your `filmed/` folder. Posting is a separate 10-minute task.

## Quality bar (before you export)

- Hook is the FIRST thing heard — no "hey guys", no logo
- One idea per script, not three squeezed together
- One freebie reference max (pinned comment or bio)
- No watermark, no stolen clips, no fake stats

## If a script feels dead

Don't fix it on batch day. Fix the **template** once, in review:
- hook style change (question → command → curiosity)
- topic change (same hook, new pain)
- CTA change ("save this" → "want the template?")

One change, then re-batch 10. The loop stays.
