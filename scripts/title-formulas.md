# Title & On-Screen Text Formulas

## Title formulas (YouTube Shorts / TikTok caption)

1. **The Fix:** "The [X-minute] [thing] that fixes [pain]"
   → "The 15-minute Sunday ritual that fixes your week"
2. **The Swap:** "Stop [bad habit]. Do [small action] instead."
   → "Stop buying planners. Use this free one-page reset instead."
3. **The Rule:** "The [number]-[number]-[number] rule for [result]"
   → "The 1-3-5 rule for a realistic day"
4. **The Cost:** "How [who] get [result] (no [money/app/course])"
   → "How broke students get organized (no course, no app)"
5. **The Claim + Proof:** "[Result] in [time] — here's exactly how"
   → "A calmer week in 15 minutes — here's exactly how"

**Keyword rule:** same 3–5 keywords in title + first spoken line + on-screen text
(e.g. "weekly reset", "calm week", "one page").

## On-screen text blocks (big, one line at a time)

- Hook line: first 2 seconds, 2–3 words if possible
- Step lines: 3–5 words each
- Freebie line: "free template → pinned comment"
- CTA line: "save this" / "follow for calm weeks"

## Thumbnail / cover formulas (later, for long-form)

- Face or object left, 3–5 words right, one contrasting color
- Never more than 5 words
- Match the title's first 3 keywords

## Pin-comment formula

> Free [thing] → link in bio. Full [pack] when it's ready — follow so you don't miss it.

Reply to every comment in the first month with a question back
("what's your week look like?"). That's the 10% that compounds.
